Hazardous Algae Removal System

May 2023 - Aug 2023

Fluid MechanicsSolar Power3D Printing Risk TestingPublic Poster
Project objective: reduce harmful algae bloom conditions by designing a solar-powered circulation system that could bring cooler water from 13-15 m depths toward the surface.

Introduction

Harmful algae blooms can damage ecosystems, reduce water quality, and create public-health concerns. Because blooms are encouraged by warm surface water, the project explored whether cooler deep water could be circulated upward as a practical mitigation strategy.

Design Process

The design focused on moving water efficiently while staying simple enough to prototype. I applied fluid mechanics to reason about flow path, losses, and circulation needs. The team used 3D-printed prototypes to test geometry choices, then refined the system based on fit, flow behavior, and validation findings.

Evaluation

Risk and validation testing helped identify practical limits of the concept, including whether prototype parts could support the intended flow and whether the system could be explained clearly to a public audience. The final project was presented as a poster to students, faculty, and visitors, which tested both the engineering logic and the clarity of the communication.

My Contribution

  • Applied fluid mechanics principles to improve flow and reduce losses.
  • Built and tested 3D-printed prototypes, then refined the design from test feedback.
  • Contributed to risk assessment, validation testing, and public project-poster communication.
