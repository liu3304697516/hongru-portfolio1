Heat Sink Design for Heat Transfer

May 2023 - Aug 2023

NX CADHeat TransferConduction ConvectionDesign Iteration
Project objective: design and optimize a heat sink that could regulate heater temperature while staying practical to model, compare, and refine through engineering iteration.

Introduction

Thermal management is essential in mechanical and electronic systems because excess temperature can reduce performance, shorten component life, and create safety risks. This project focused on a heat sink as a compact way to move heat away from a source through geometry and material choices.

Design Process

I used heat transfer principles to guide the design, considering conduction through the base, convection from fin surfaces, and material properties that affect thermal response. NX models were used to compare geometry options and make each iteration more specific than the last.

Evaluation

The design was evaluated through performance iteration: compare a geometry, identify why temperature regulation could improve, and revise the model. This process made the final design more effective for thermal management while keeping the geometry understandable and manufacturable.

My Contribution

  • Modeled heat sink geometries in NX based on conduction, convection, and material-property reasoning.
  • Compared design iterations to improve thermal regulation.
  • Refined fin and base geometry for better heat dissipation and manufacturability.
  • Connected design choices to the practical goal of stable heater temperature control.
